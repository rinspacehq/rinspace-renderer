\section{MathJax reader contract}

The native negated-exists glyph must preserve its original source:
$\not\exists k\in\mathbb{R}$.

The dynamic double-struck font table must remain available:
$\forall c_i, c_i\in\mathbb{R}$.

The column vector must keep its matrix source unwrapped and use stretchy delimiters:
\[
\mathbf{x}=\begin{bmatrix}
a_1\\
a_2\\
\cdots\\
a_n
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

The alignment group must remain one display math unit:
\begin{align*}
\mathbf{x}_1+\mathbf{x}_2 &= \begin{bmatrix}a_1+b_1\\a_2+b_2\end{bmatrix}.
\end{align*}
